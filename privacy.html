Herald is a personal AI companion, not a medical device, healthcare provider, or licensed medical professional. Herald does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition or disease. Nothing Herald says should be relied upon as medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for any medical concerns. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately.

Important — Not financial advice

Herald is not a registered investment advisor, broker-dealer, financial planner, or financial institution. Herald does not provide investment advice, financial planning, tax advice, or recommendations to buy or sell any security, asset, or financial product. Any financial information discussed within Herald is for general informational purposes only based on what you have shared, and does not constitute professional financial advice. Nothing Herald says should be relied upon as a basis for any financial or investment decision. Always consult a qualified financial professional before making financial decisions.

1. Information we collect

Herald collects information you provide directly through conversation and onboarding:

  • Your name, location, and email address (if provided)
  • Preferences and interests you share in conversation
  • Life events, appointments, and reminders you ask Herald to track
  • Health-related topics you mention conversationally (not stored as medical records)
  • Financial topics you mention conversationally (not stored as financial records)
  • Device location data (GPS coordinates, used to provide local context)
  • Usage data including messages sent and features used

We do not collect payment card information, government ID numbers, or Social Security numbers.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and personalize the Herald experience
  • Remember context across conversations so Herald can help you more effectively
  • Send proactive reminders, briefings, and alerts you have requested
  • Improve and develop Herald's features and capabilities
  • Communicate with you about your account and Herald updates
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. What Herald stores — and what it does not

Herald stores conversational context — things you tell us — to make the experience personal and useful. This is different from clinical or financial records.

  • Herald does not store your medical records or act as a healthcare records system
  • Herald does not store financial account numbers, brokerage credentials, or tax records
  • Herald does not access your bank accounts, investment accounts, or credit information
  • Herald does not share your personal information with advertisers
  • Herald does not sell your data. Ever.

Conversational data (what you share with Herald) is stored securely on servers in the United States. It is used only to power your Herald experience.

4. Location data

With your permission, Herald accesses your device's GPS location to provide local context such as weather, nearby recommendations, and location-aware reminders. Location data is used in real time and stored only as a general city-level label to improve future responses. We do not track your precise location history or share your location with third parties.
