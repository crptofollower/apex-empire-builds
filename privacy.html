1. What Herald is trying to be

Herald is a Personal AI you talk to. Conversation is the experience. Memory is the product: the meaningful things about your life, so you do not have to begin again every time you speak.

Personal memories belong on your device. That is the direction of the product. We do not claim, in this policy, a specific technical guarantee we cannot show you from this website. We will not describe your life as a cloud profile, and we will not use it as an advertising asset.

Information from the wider world may be used when it is helpful. It should not become the authority over your personal life.

That list is separate from Herald’s personal memory. Joining it does not mean we hold the story of your life. It means we can reach you about early access.

We do not sell the early access list. We do not use it to advertise other people’s products to you.
